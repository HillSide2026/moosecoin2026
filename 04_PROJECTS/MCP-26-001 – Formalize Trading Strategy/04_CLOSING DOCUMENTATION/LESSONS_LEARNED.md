# Lessons Learned / Retrospective

## What Worked

- Formal scope narrowing increased clarity and reduced execution ambiguity.
- Separating principles, strategy, and playbook improved auditability.

## What Didn’t

- Stage documentation initially diverged from schema; required later reconciliation.

## Improvements

- Establish a template compliance checklist at project start.
- Maintain decision backlog as part of Stage 1 outputs.
