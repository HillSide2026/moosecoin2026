# Trading Principles (Execution Stage)

1. **Capital Comes First**  
Capital preservation is the primary objective.  
All opportunity is secondary.

2. **Not Trading Is the Default State**  
The strategy assumes most market states are untradable.  
Activity requires justification; inactivity does not.

3. **Trades Must Earn the Right to Exist**  
A trade is a liability until proven otherwise.  
Eligibility gates precede any setup logic.

4. **Regimes Matter More Than Signals**  
Trades are allowed only in specific market regimes.  
Signals outside valid regimes are ignored by design.

5. **Transitional Regimes Only**  
Dormant regimes: do nothing.  
Dislocated regimes: do nothing.  
Transitional regimes: only place where risk may be taken.

6. **Liquidity Is a First-Order Risk**  
If liquidity or spreads degrade, the strategy stands aside.  
Execution quality is not a cost—it is risk.

7. **Risk Capacity Overrides Opportunity**  
When risk capacity is impaired, trading stops.  
There are no recovery trades and no urgency exceptions.

8. **Hard Constraints Are Non-Negotiable**  
No averaging down.  
No position scaling.  
No discretionary overrides.  
No exceptions.

9. **Strategy Failure Triggers Suspension, Not Optimization**  
When assumptions fail, the strategy is turned off, not tweaked.  
Reactivation requires review and approval.

10. **Process Fidelity Beats Performance**  
The strategy is judged first on:  
Rule adherence.  
Risk compliance.  
Discipline.  
P&L is a consequence, not a control variable.

11. **One Strategy, One Context**  
Only one active strategy instance per account.  
Complexity is a risk vector.

12. **Standing Aside Is a Positive Action**  
Choosing not to trade is an intentional, correct outcome, not a miss.

13. **Execution Must Be Enforceable**  
If a rule cannot be enforced technically or procedurally,  
the trade is not allowed to exist.

14. **Change Is Expensive**  
Any change requires:  
Rationale.  
Impact analysis.  
Approval.  
Risk-increasing changes require capital-owner consent.

15. **Auditability Is Mandatory**  
Every trade must be explainable, reconstructible, and version-bound.  
If it can’t be audited, it doesn’t belong.
