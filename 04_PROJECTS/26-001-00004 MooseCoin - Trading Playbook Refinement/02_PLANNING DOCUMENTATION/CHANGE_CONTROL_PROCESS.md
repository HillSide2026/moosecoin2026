# Change Control Process

## Purpose
Ensure all refinements to the trading playbook are explicit, traceable, and ML1‑approved.

## Change Workflow
1. **Propose** change with a clear rationale and source artifact reference.
2. **Assess** downstream impact (platform selection, execution readiness, governance).
3. **Record** change in `CHANGE_LOG_DELTA_NOTES.md`.
4. **Approve** by ML1 before incorporation.

## Required Fields for Any Change
- What changed
- Why (source artifact)
- Impact on downstream projects
- Approval status

## Freeze Conditions
- Any ambiguity in strategy intent
- Conflicting source artifacts
- Changes that risk Layer 3 leakage

## Approval Authority
- ML1 only
