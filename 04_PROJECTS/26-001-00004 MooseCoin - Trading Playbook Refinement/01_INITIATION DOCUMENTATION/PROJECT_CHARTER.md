# Project Charter

## Purpose
Define and govern Project 26-001-00004 to refine and extend the MooseCoin trading playbook based on validated outputs from Project 26-001-00001.

## Objective
Produce a consolidated, internally consistent, execution‑ready Layer 2 playbook that is upstream of platform selection and execution.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Refinement of trading principles and constraints
- Extension of Layer 2 strategy definition content
- Incorporation of validation findings from Project 26-001-00001
- Consolidation of fragmented strategy artifacts
- Versioning and change tracking of playbook documents

### Out of Scope
- Platform selection or evaluation (Project 26-001-00002)
- Platform configuration/automation design
- Website/marketing/public content (Project 26-001-00003)
- Creation of execution setups (Layer 3)
- Live trading, testing, or capital deployment

## Success Criteria
- Playbook is internally consistent and contradiction‑free
- Clear separation of governance (Layer 1) vs strategy (Layer 2) vs execution (Layer 3)
- All changes traced to Project 26-001-00001 outputs
- Open questions are explicit and routed to ML1

## Authority & Approvals
- ML1 is sole decision-maker and approver
- ML2/agents may draft only

## Dependencies
- Project 26-001-00001 (Trading Strategy) outputs

## Deliverables (Draft)
- Updated Trading Playbook version(s)
- Refined Layer 2 strategy documentation
- Consolidated principles list
- Change log and version diffs
- Open questions list for ML1
