# Strategy Invariants (Non-Negotiables)

## Why It Exists
Defines the rules that can never be broken, regardless of conviction.

## Invariants
- No trading outside Transitional regimes
- No discretionary overrides
- No scaling or averaging
- Standing aside is valid
