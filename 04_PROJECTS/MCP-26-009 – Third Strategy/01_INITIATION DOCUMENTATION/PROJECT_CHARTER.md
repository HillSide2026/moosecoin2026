# Project Charter

## Purpose

Establish governed initiation and decision framing for Third Strategy.

## Objective

Produce ML1 decision-ready initiation outputs that define scope, constraints, and go/no-go criteria.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Problem framing and boundaries
- Dependencies and blockers
- Initial risk and assumption capture

### Out of Scope

- Unapproved stage advancement
- Live deployment decisions

## Success Criteria

- Mandatory initiation documents complete
- Dependencies and constraints explicitly documented
- ML1 decision package prepared

## Authority & Approvals

- ML1 is sole decision-maker and approver
- ML2/agents draft and analyze only
