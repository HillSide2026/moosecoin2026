# Change Control Process

## Change Triggers

- Proposed modification to gating checklist criteria
- Proposed adjustment to evaluation methodology
- Proposed change to platform shortlist or evaluation scope

## Process

1. Change request documented with rationale
2. Impact analysis (risk, scope, schedule)
3. Risk authority review
4. ML1 approval required
5. Versioned update recorded

## Principle

No change is allowed that weakens enforcement of the gating checklist or introduces workaround logic.
