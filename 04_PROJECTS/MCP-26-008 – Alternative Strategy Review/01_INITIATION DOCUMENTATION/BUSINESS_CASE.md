# Business Case

## Why This Project Exists

A single strategy path increases concentration risk. Reviewing alternatives improves resilience and decision quality before committing model and execution resources.

## Problem Statement

Without a structured alternative review process, strategy selection can become path-dependent and vulnerable to untested assumptions.

## Expected Value

- Preserve optionality at the strategy layer
- Identify structurally superior or more robust candidates
- Reduce rework by screening for governance and implementability early

## Cost of Inaction

- Over-commitment to a single design path
- Higher downstream rework if assumptions fail
- Slower response to regime mismatch in live conditions

## Decision Standard

Alternative candidates are evaluated using pass/fail governance and risk gates before any preference-based ranking.

## Recommendation

Proceed with controlled initiation: define candidate criteria, screen alternatives, and present ML1 with a decision package.
