# Project Charter

## Purpose

Create a governed process to review one or more alternative strategy candidates without compromising doctrine, risk controls, or auditability.

## Objective

Produce an ML1 decision-ready recommendation on whether an alternative strategy candidate should advance to planning and formal specification.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Candidate strategy framing and hypothesis definition
- Regime and risk-constraint compatibility screening
- Determinism and enforceability checks
- Recommendation memo with explicit decision points

### Out of Scope

- Profitability claims or optimization work
- Backtesting and live deployment
- Execution system implementation
- Doctrine approval decisions

## Success Criteria

- At least one alternative candidate is documented with clear thesis, regime logic, and failure conditions
- Candidate passes initial governance and risk screen
- Escalations and open decisions are explicitly surfaced for ML1

## Authority & Approvals

- ML1 is sole decision-maker and approver
- ML2/agents may draft, analyze, and report only

## Deliverables (Initiation-Level)

- Business case
- High-level requirements summary
- Initial risk register (lite)
- Candidate review decision memo (draft)
