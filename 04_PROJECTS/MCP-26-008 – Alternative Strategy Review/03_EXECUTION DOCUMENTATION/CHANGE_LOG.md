# CHANGE_LOG

Every change must be recorded. No silent modifications.

## Change Entries

| Date | Strategy Version | Change Summary | Rationale | Risk Impact | Approval Status |
|------|------------------|----------------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| YYYY-MM-DD | vX.Y | TBD | TBD | neutral \| increasing \| decreasing | draft \| approved \| rejected |

## Rules

- `Risk Impact` is mandatory for each change.
- `Approval Status` must be explicit.
- Changes with `risk impact = increasing` require explicit ML1 approval before use.
