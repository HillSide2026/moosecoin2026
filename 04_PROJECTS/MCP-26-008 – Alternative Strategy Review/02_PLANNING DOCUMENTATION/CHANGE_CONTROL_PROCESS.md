# Change Control Process

## Change Triggers

- Proposed changes to regime definitions or thresholds
- Proposed changes to spike, stall-confirmation, or invalidation rules
- Proposed adjustments to sizing, participation caps, or time-stop behavior
- Proposed relaxation of no-trade filters or risk constraints
- Proposed structural change to strategy-formalization artifacts

## Process

1. Document change request with rationale and exact artifact references.
2. Complete impact analysis across risk, scope, auditability, and implementation complexity.
3. Validate alignment against `01_DOCTRINE/` constraints and project scope.
4. Escalate unresolved ambiguity or conflicts to ML1 with decision-needed notes.
5. Require explicit ML1 approval before any accepted update.
6. Record approved changes with versioned artifact updates and decision log entries.

## Principle

No change is allowed that increases discretionary behavior, weakens risk controls, or reduces reconstructability.
