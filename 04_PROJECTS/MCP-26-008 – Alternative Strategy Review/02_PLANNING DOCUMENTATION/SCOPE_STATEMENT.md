# Scope Statement

## In Scope

- Formalize STRAT3 planning artifacts for governance-compliant review
- Define objective regime, spike, stall, sizing, and exit rule boundaries
- Validate deterministic enforceability of STRAT3 logic before implementation
- Define planning-stage decision package for ML1 go/no-go
- Define handoff requirements from project planning artifacts to `02_MODELS/`

## Out of Scope

- Live or paper trading
- Broker/platform execution integration
- Automation deployment
- Doctrine publication or authority changes
- Profitability claims or optimization loops

## Assumptions

- STRAT3 remains a candidate until explicit ML1 advancement decision
- Fix-window behavior can be represented with objective thresholds
- Low participation is acceptable when quality gates are strict
- Ambiguity must resolve to no-trade behavior

## Constraints

- No discretionary override paths
- No weakening of doctrine-aligned risk constraints
- All rules must be objectively testable and auditable
- Every planning artifact must preserve traceability to strategy intent/spec
- Outputs remain non-authoritative until ML1 approval
