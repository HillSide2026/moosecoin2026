# Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Identify

**Goal:** Define the project mandate and success criteria.

**Outputs**
- Problem statement and scope boundaries
- Stakeholders and decision owners
- Success metrics and constraints

**Milestones**
- Stage 1 kickoff complete
- Stage 1 outputs approved

## Stage 2 — Roadmap

**Goal:** Translate the mandate into a sequenced plan.

**Outputs**
- Milestones and delivery timeline
- Dependencies and risks
- Resource plan and assumptions

**Milestones**
- Roadmap draft complete
- Roadmap review and sign-off

## Stage 3 — Backlog

**Goal:** Build the prioritized work queue.

**Outputs**
- Backlog items with scope, effort, and priority
- Definition of ready/definition of done
- Grooming cadence and owner

**Milestones**
- Initial backlog populated
- Prioritization rubric approved

## Stage 4 — Next Steps / Next Sprints

**Goal:** Commit to the next execution window.

**Outputs**
- Next sprint goals and deliverables
- Owners and due dates
- Weekly check-in schedule

**Milestones**
- Sprint plan confirmed
- First sprint kickoff complete
