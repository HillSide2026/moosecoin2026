# Analysis

## Objectives

- Provide a clear, repeatable flow for project intake and delivery.
- Make stage transitions explicit so stakeholders can assess readiness.
- Keep backlog and next sprint commitments visible and current.

## Risks

- Ambiguous ownership of stage gates could stall progress.
- Backlog may grow without prioritization criteria.

## Recommendations

- Assign a single accountable owner for stage transitions.
- Use a lightweight prioritization rubric (impact, effort, dependency).
- Review backlog and next steps on a fixed cadence.
