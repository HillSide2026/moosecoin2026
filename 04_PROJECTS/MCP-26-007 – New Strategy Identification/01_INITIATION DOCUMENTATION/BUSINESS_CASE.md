# Business Case

## Why This Project Exists

Strategy 1 has been archived. ML2 now requires a replacement strategy direction that preserves governance discipline while improving implementability.

## Problem Statement

Without a current active strategy, downstream model and execution work lacks a valid strategic anchor.

## Expected Value

- Re-establish an active strategic direction for `02_MODELS/`
- Reduce ambiguity in model and protocol design
- Improve implementation readiness by selecting strategy forms that are enforceable and auditable

## Cost of Inaction

- Model development stalls or drifts
- Platform and execution decisions lose strategic context
- Governance overhead increases due to repeated ambiguity

## Decision Standard

Candidate selection is pass/fail against predefined constraints, not preference-based ranking.

## Recommendation

Proceed with strategy identification project in controlled phases:
1. Candidate generation
2. Constraint screening
3. Selection and handoff to modeling
