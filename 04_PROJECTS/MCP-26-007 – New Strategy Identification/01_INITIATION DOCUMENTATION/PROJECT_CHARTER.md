# Project Charter

## Purpose

Establish a governed process to identify a new strategy candidate that is risk-coherent, auditable, and implementable inside ML2.

## Objective

Produce one primary strategy candidate with a clear regime thesis, risk assumptions, and decision-engine readiness for downstream modeling.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Define strategy design constraints and acceptance gates
- Generate and compare multiple strategy candidates
- Select one primary candidate and one backup candidate
- Document rationale, risks, and unresolved decisions

### Out of Scope

- Live trading and capital deployment
- Broker/platform execution implementation
- Doctrine approval and publication

## Success Criteria

- A primary candidate is selected with explicit go/no-go rationale
- Candidate has clear regime definition, entry/exit logic concept, and risk posture
- Candidate can be represented in `02_MODELS/` as a decision engine
- Open uncertainties are recorded as ML1 decisions, not hidden assumptions

## Authority & Approvals

- ML1 is sole decision-maker and approver
- ML2/agents draft and analyze only

## Deliverables (Initiation-Level)

- Business case for strategy refresh
- High-level requirements summary
- Initial risk register (lite)
- Candidate selection criteria and decision memo (draft)
