# Strategy Formalization Plan

## Purpose

Define the planning-stage work required to convert the Strategy 2 candidate into a model-ready, governance-compliant strategy specification.

## Planning Workstreams

1. Regime formalization
2. Rule precision and ambiguity removal
3. Candidate screening and selection logic
4. Validation design and evidence requirements
5. Handoff package for downstream model drafting

## Exit Criteria for Planning

- Regime states and thresholds are measurable
- Entry, invalidation, sizing, and exit logic are deterministic
- No-trade filters are explicit and testable
- Candidate selection criteria are frozen
- Validation protocol is approved for execution-stage use
