# Trading Principles (Shared)

This document defines shared principles governing all strategy systems under development.

## 1. Fail-Fast Doctrine

Structural failure exits are immediate and non-discretionary.

## 2. No Anticipation Rule

No pre-trigger positioning, no predictive early entries.

## 3. Regime > Signal Hierarchy

Signals are valid only inside approved regimes.

## 4. Survival > Frequency

Capital preservation and risk coherence outrank trade count.

## 5. Missing Trades Is Acceptable

Inactivity is a valid and often preferred outcome.

## 6. Ambiguity Defaults to No Trade

If conditions are unclear or inputs are incomplete, stand aside.

## 7. Enforceability Requirement

If a rule cannot be enforced technically/procedurally, it is not deployable.

## 8. Auditability Requirement

Every trade decision must be reconstructible from logged state and versioned logic.
