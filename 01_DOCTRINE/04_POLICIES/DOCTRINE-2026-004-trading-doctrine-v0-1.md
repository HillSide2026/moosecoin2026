# Trading Doctrine v0.1

**Status:** Draft – Not Approved  
**Owner:** ML1 (Matthew Levine)  
**System of Record:** ML2 (Second Brain)

---

## Purpose

This doctrine defines the **non-negotiable constraints** under which all trading activity occurs.

It applies across strategies, asset classes, market regimes, and tools.

The doctrine makes **no claims of edge, foresight, optimization, or necessity of participation**. Its sole function is to preserve capital, decision quality, and long-term optionality.

---

## Foundational Principles

1. **Capital and Optionality Supremacy**
   The preservation of capital and future decision-making capacity takes precedence over return generation in all circumstances.

2. **Human Authority Over Automated Processes**
   Automated systems may be used to observe, structure, model, or execute, but **final authority over exposure, risk, and continuation rests exclusively with ML1**.

3. **No Mandate to Participate**
   There is no obligation to trade.
   Periods of inactivity are valid, expected, and often optimal.

4. **Survivability as a Hard Constraint**
   Every position must be survivable under adverse conditions without impairing future judgment, liquidity, or operating capacity.

---

## Authority and Responsibility

### ML1 (Human Authority)

ML1 alone retains authority to:

* Decide whether to engage the market
* Select instruments and directional exposure
* Approve or override automated signals and executions
* Determine position size, leverage, and timing
* Terminate exposure irrespective of system state
* Accept full responsibility for outcomes

Accountability is not delegated.

---

### ML2 (System Role)

ML2 functions strictly as a **supporting and recording mechanism**. It may:

* Prepare data, signals, or structured context
* Document intent, assumptions, and outcomes
* Preserve historical decisions and post-trade records

ML2 does **not**:

* Initiate trading activity independently
* Enforce exposure continuation
* Substitute for human judgment
* Override ML1 authority

---

## Risk Posture

* Risk is deliberate and bounded.
* Leverage is avoided by default and treated as an exception requiring explicit justification.
* Complexity—technical, structural, or conceptual—is treated as a risk factor.

Risk may be expressed qualitatively or quantitatively at ML1’s discretion.

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

This doctrine does not assert:

* The existence of a durable or exploitable market edge
* Superior forecasting or informational advantage
* That activity improves outcomes
* That markets must be traded

Participation remains contingent.

## Change Control

* Amendments require explicit approval by ML1
* All changes are versioned and archived
* Doctrinal changes are expected to be infrequent and deliberate
