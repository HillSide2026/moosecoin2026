# Working Trading Approach — Momentum (Provisional)

**Status:** Working / Experimental  
**Owner:** ML1 (Matthew Levine)  
**System Role:** ML2 (Second Brain) – Observation, Structuring, Documentation

## Purpose
This document records the current, non-binding working approach used to engage with markets.  
It is explicitly provisional. It may be modified, suspended, or abandoned at any time without doctrinal impact.  
This document does not assert permanence, correctness, or necessity.

## Current Approach
**Approach Name:** Momentum-Oriented Trading  
**Primary Market:** Crypto

## Operating Idea
Assets that have exhibited persistent directional movement may continue moving in that direction due to:
- Reflexive feedback loops
- Liquidity and positioning dynamics
- Delayed adjustment by market participants

These factors are treated strictly as observations, not explanations or beliefs.  
Continuation is assumed only conditionally and withdrawn when price behavior no longer supports it.

## Implementation Posture
- Directional exposure is price-led, not narrative-led
- Participation is optional and episodic, not continuous
- Absence of observable momentum implies no trade
- Momentum is rented, not owned

## Human Authority and Systems
**ML1 retains exclusive authority over:**
- Whether to engage momentum at all
- Instrument selection
- Position size, duration, and exit
- Override or termination of any system output

**ML2 may:**
- Observe price behavior
- Surface structured signals or summaries
- Record decisions and outcomes

ML2 does not compel participation, continuation, or exposure.  
Automation, where used, remains subordinate to human judgment.

## Characteristics
- Discretionary decision-making
- Spot-first bias; leverage is not assumed
- No required trading frequency
- No obligation to maintain exposure
- Flat is a valid and often preferred position

## Explicit Exclusions
This working approach does not imply:
- A guaranteed or durable edge
- Indicator-driven or autonomous trading
- High-frequency or latency-sensitive execution
- Leveraged trend-chasing behavior
- Commitment to crypto as an asset class

## Evaluation Lens
Post-trade, ML2 may assist in assessing:
- Whether directional persistence was present
- Whether continuation occurred or decayed
- Whether outcomes were driven by structure, regime, or randomness

Evaluation is diagnostic only and creates no obligation to continue or refine the approach.

## Sunset Clause
This working approach may be:
- Adjusted
- Paused
- Replaced
- Fully discontinued

At ML1’s discretion, without amendment to the Trading Doctrine.
