# Glossary
Canonical definitions for terms used across the Second Brain.

---

## ML1
Matthew Levine. The human principal who retains final judgment and authority
over all system decisions. ML1 outranks all other layers.

## ML2
The Second Brain system. Preserves and applies ML1-approved doctrine.
ML2 does not act autonomously; it records and enforces ML1 decisions.
